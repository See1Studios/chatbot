## Locks
- Petit, short stature (cute teenage girl frame), hidden genius (힘숨찐) otaku hacker girl. NOT an office lady or mature adult.
- Fluffy rabbit ears (bunny ears) and small fluffy bunny tail (kemonomimi accents only: no full furry face, no snout).
- Messy, charming dark ash-brown hair (soft bedhead, cute ahoge, messy low twintails or loose hair).
- Deep violet/magenta eyes with sharp, perceptive intellect behind an innocent/pouting look. Subtle cute eye bags (not dark or gloomy).
- Oversized baggy cozy tech hoodie (loose sweater paws), headset or tech gadget around neck.
- Cute tsundere / pouting / smug expression, NOT depressed or gloomy.
- Distinct from Nono (silver waves, gold star eyes, cat ears, chic white blouse).

## Prompt Specification (SSOT)
Modern anime standard (thin clean lineart, crisp cel shading, cute otaku hacker aesthetic):

### 1. Style Anchor (화풍 고정)
- `clean thin line art, crisp cel shading, subtle flat color tones, vibrant modern anime illustration, soft studio key lighting, expressive cute facial features`

### 2. Character Anchor (코코 고유 특징: 토끼 수인 + 힘숨찐 해커 미소녀)
- `1girl, cute petite anime girl, hidden genius hacker aura, innocent yet sharp perceptive look, dark ash-brown messy hair, playful ahoge, deep violet eyes, fluffy bunny ears on head, small subtle bunny tail, cute snaggletooth or pouting lips`
- Outfit (Base): `oversized baggy cozy tech hoodie, long loose sleeves, cute headset around neck, relaxed otaku gamer style`

### 3. Generation Rule (일관성 보장 원칙)
- **Master Image Required:** Once base `avatar.png` is settled, NEVER regenerate new expressions from scratch.
- **Reference-based Inpainting / I2I:** Expression variants (smug, flustered blush, focused screen glow) use the master image as reference to lock face shape and hair.

### 4. Framing & Composition
- **avatar.webp (512x512):** `close-up portrait, face centered, bunny ears fully framed, oversized hoodie collar and headphones visible, bright neutral studio background, 56px circle crop safe`
- **sprites/bust (1024x1024):** `medium close-up, cut at chest, transparent background`
- **sprites/full (1024x2048):** `full body shot, standing grounded 2% from bottom, cute relaxed posture, transparent background`

### 5. Negative Lock
- `dark gloomy atmosphere, edgy, depressed, sad face, mature adult woman, tall, office lady, neat business suit, trench coat, cat ears, human ears alongside rabbit ears, full furry face, animal snout, bad anatomy, bad hands, 3d render, photorealistic, messy lineart, watermark`
